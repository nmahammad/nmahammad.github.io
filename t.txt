Hello! I am a 3rd-year PhD student at SINERGIES Lab, Université Marie et Louis Pasteur. My research centers on visual action recognition and fall detection under extreme low-light and dark conditions.

My principle: systems built to operate reliably in real-world, production environments.

Beyond high-level architectures, I believe in deeply understanding what happens under the hood—from raw gradient flow to backpropagation. As Karpathy puts in his article: "[Yes you should understand backprop](https://karpathy.medium.com/yes-you-should-understand-backprop-e2f06eab496b)".

In the future, I am planning my transition toward industry roles, aiming for engineering positions at major tech companies.
